Our Record

Public Order Initiative

Championed and passed the District Security and Civil Order Act, strengthening patrol coordination and restoring confidence in public spaces. Crime incidents in core sectors have declined measurably since enactment.

Economic Prudence Act

Introduced strict fiscal oversight measures and a new procurement review board. Reduced wasteful spending by over 14% in the last fiscal year while protecting essential services.

Heritage Preservation Ordinance

Our Platform

Law & Order

We believe a functioning society requires visible, fair, and consistent enforcement of the law. We will expand community watch programs and invest in professional training for District security forces.

Merit & Responsibility

Opportunity must be earned through contribution and character. We support apprenticeship programs, trade education, and incentives for citizens who demonstrate commitment to the common good.

Fiscal Discipline

Sound money and restrained government are the foundation of lasting prosperity. We will continue to oppose reckless spending and push for transparent budgeting at every level.

Cultural Continuity

A city is more than infrastructure. We defend the customs, institutions and shared memory that bind generations. We will protect religious and cultural expression while promoting civic pride.

Optimate Leaders

Senior Councillor Aurelius Varrus
PARTY CHAIRMAN

Aurelius Varrus descends from one of the founding administrative families of City 13. Educated in classical governance and jurisprudence, he served fifteen years in the Civil Affairs Bureau before entering elected office.

A vocal proponent of structured hierarchy and institutional integrity, Councillor Varrus has authored multiple ordinances reinforcing property rights and professional standards in public service. He was elected Party Chairman in 2007.

Varrus maintains that "excellence is not a birthright — it is a duty we renew each day."

District Councillor Regina Holt

Regina Holt rose through the ranks of the Municipal Treasury, eventually becoming Deputy Comptroller. Her reputation for uncompromising audits and zero tolerance for waste made her a natural ally of the Optimate cause.

First elected in 2006, she has focused on reforming public contracts and restoring public trust in municipal finance. She is widely regarded as the party’s most effective legislative tactician.

District Councillor Commander Silas Korr

A decorated veteran of the pre-Accord security forces, Commander Korr brings decades of experience in maintaining civil order under the most difficult conditions. He joined the Optimate movement shortly after the formation of District 7.

Councillor Korr champions practical reforms to the District constabulary, emphasizing discipline, accountability, and the dignity of public service. He is a frequent speaker on civic duty and personal responsibility.
