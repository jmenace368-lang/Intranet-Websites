New Labour. New Life.

The Combine Labour Co-operative delivers for the working people. Labour has changed the city for the better, with achievements that have made a lasting difference to people’s lives across our city.

From rebuilding homes to securing fairer rations and safer workplaces, Labour was formed to give ordinary people a voice and improve lives. For the past several years, we stood for practical change that improves the lives of our city's residents, and now, we aim for greater goals in District 7.

What we've delivered

Housing Reform

As part of a popular, multi-party legislative initiative, we joined others in introducing comprehensive legislation to officially segregate Biotic and Human housing zones, establishing a Biotic exclusionary zone that better meets their psychological demands.

Electoral Reform

Under our initiative, we proudly sponsored landmark legislation that successfully waived the monetary credit fee for voting eligibility by default, allowing broader civic participation and incentivizing voter attendance.

Plan for Change

Social Investment

Invest in those who deserve it and are seeking to contribute to our District's productivity. Citizens who contribute to the District, whether as a Collaborator or labourer, will be duly rewarded for their ethics.

Providing Opportunities

Provide labour and business opportunities to citizens to successfully integrate into the workforce. Through our initiatives, citizens will be able to find productive, streamlined employment regardless of status or tier.
