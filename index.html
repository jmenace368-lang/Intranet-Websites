Working to build a stronger, better, and more prosperous City 13.

Join the Movement

Welcome, Citizen

Combine Labour Co-operative is the voice of the working people across City 13. We believe in solidarity, progress, and the right of every citizen to a life of dignity under the Combine.

From rebuilding homes to securing fairer rations and safer workplaces, our movement stands for practical change that improves the daily lives of families in every district.

Our Mission

To unite workers, families, and communities in building a fairer, stronger City 13 where everyone contributes and everyone benefits.

Join Us

Whether you work in the factories, maintain the infrastructure, or raise the next generation, there is a place for you in the movement.

What we've delivered

Housing Reform

As part of a popular, multi-party legislative initiative, we joined others in introducing comprehensive legislation to officially segregate Biotic and Human housing zones, establishing a Biotic exclusionary zone that better meets their psychological demands.

Electoral Reform

Under our initiative, we proudly sponsored landmark legislation that successfully waived the monetary credit fee for voting eligibility by default, allowing broader civic participation and incentivizing voter attendance.

Plan for Change

Universal Ration Guarantee

We will fight for a guaranteed minimum daily ration for every citizen, removing the uncertainty that comes with fluctuating work credits and production quotas.

Workplace Safety Act

Our plan introduces mandatory safety standards in all Combine facilities, including protective gear, regular inspections, and compensation for work-related injuries.

Community Reconstruction

We will expand the successful housing and infrastructure programs to cover Districts 1 through 12, creating jobs while improving living conditions for all.

Democratic Representation

Every workplace and residential block will elect local labour representatives with real influence over ration distribution and work assignments.

News & Updates

Ration Distribution Reform Passes Council

The Combine Labour proposal to stabilise weekly rations has been approved. Starting next month, all registered workers will receive a baseline credit allocation regardless of overtime performance.

New Safety Equipment Distributed in Sector 9

Over 12,000 sets of reinforced protective gear have been issued to factory workers following the successful trial of the new Combine Labour Workplace Safety Act. Early reports show a 40% drop in minor incidents.

District 4 Community Hall Reopens

After months of collaborative reconstruction between local volunteers and Combine engineering units, the District 4 Community Hall is once again open for public meetings and educational programs.

Public Forum: The Future of City 13

Join representatives from Combine Labour and other approved groups on the 18th for an open discussion on upcoming infrastructure projects and citizen welfare initiatives. Location: Central Plaza, Sector 2.
