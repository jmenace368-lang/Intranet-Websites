What we've delivered

Real, measurable change across District 7. These are some of the key wins for working people.

Housing Reform

Segregated Biotic and human zones while upgrading conditions for thousands of families.

+12,400 units improved

Electoral Reform

Removed credit fees for voting eligibility, opening participation to more citizens.

+68% voter turnout

Workplace Safety Standards

Introduced mandatory protocols and worker representation on factory floors.

-41% serious incidents

Ration Equity Programme

Guaranteed fair, high-quality allocations for all labourers and families.

98% on-time delivery

Transit & Infrastructure

New reliable lines connecting residential blocks with work districts.

4 new lines, 19 stations

Labour Rights Charter

Secured collective representation and protection from arbitrary discipline.

100k+ workers protected

Plan for Change

Five clear priorities to make District 7 the model of fairness and opportunity.

Worker Empowerment

Real voice and reward for those who contribute.

  • Expand worker councils with real say
  • Living wage indexed to living costs
  • Universal skills training & progression

Housing for All

Decent homes as a foundation, not a luxury.

  • Complete regeneration of key blocks
  • Rent stability for essential workers
  • 6,500 new family homes

Health & Wellbeing

Care that is accessible and preventative.

  • Local community health clinics
  • Universal mental health support
  • Better nutrition in rations

Sustainable Growth

Investment that lasts for generations.

  • Green industrial retrofits
  • Circular economy pilot
  • Local food and water security

Youth & Future

Opportunity for the next generation.

  • Apprenticeships with job guarantees
  • New technical academies
  • Youth voice at the table
