Plan for Change

Our second-term agenda focuses on deepening democracy in the workplace and accelerating the just transition. Four interlocking pillars guide everything we do.
🏠

Living Wage Guarantee

Every full-time worker will earn a true living wage. Automatic annual adjustments tied to housing costs and inflation. No exceptions for contractors.

🤝

Co-operative Expansion

Fund and scale democratic worker co-ops in every district. Ownership and profit-sharing belong to the people who do the work. New matching grants and legal support.

🌱

Green Renewal Project

Massive city-wide retrofits for clean energy, zero-carbon transit and climate-resilient neighbourhoods. 180,000 new green jobs targeted by 2010.

❤️

Universal Care Network

Integrated healthcare, childcare and elder support — free at the point of use. Expanding to full coverage for mental health and reproductive care.
